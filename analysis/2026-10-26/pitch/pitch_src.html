Zenith · Western Australia's electricity market

Is 1 kWh in a customer's home battery worth 70c to Zenith?

Every morning at 09:00, Zenith must decide: call its customers' batteries for tonight's peak, or leave them alone.

The original challenge is to forecast prices (Q2A) and run a battery across markets (Q2B). That engine is what answers this question.

Step 1 · the original challenge

In-sample: the days used to build and choose the method, so its gain is not a promise about the future. Test: days after 30 Jun 2026, reported only. The last 7 days had not been looked at before.

Step 2 · the answer

Most days, no. On a few peak days, by far.

Why only peak days?

Zenith's share of the grid's capacity cost, the Individual Reserve Capacity Requirement (IRCR), is set by the grid's 4 highest-demand days each summer. A kWh exported in those hours cuts a cost Zenith pays for a whole year. On other days a kWh is worth only its energy price.
